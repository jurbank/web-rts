# Advanced utopian

## Identity

An advanced faction running on clean energy. Its buildings are sleek: single sweeping forms of glossy white shell and lit glass, with large-radius curves, roofs that float past the walls, and nothing bolted on. Think of a Gulf skyline's sail towers and a minimalist tech campus's glass rings and seamless shells, without copying any real building. Each building sits on a clean landscaped plaza. It is spotless: no dirt, grime, stains, or wear anywhere, on its buildings or its units. It glows softly from within, never in hard neon lines. Whatever map it stands on, it should look like the most advanced thing there. No buildings are approved yet, so the first ones set the look provisionally.

## Shape language

- **One gesture per building.** Each building is one bold, continuous form, such as a ring, a lens, a sail, or a pill-shaped shell, plus at most one secondary element.
- **Large-radius curves.** Corners and edges are generously rounded, and curves run unbroken around the mass. No steps, setbacks, bands, or visible joints between parts.
- **Glass skins under white shells.** Walls are glass curtains with fine vertical white fins. Smooth white shells and roofs sweep past the glass and overhang it.
- **Floating roofs.** Thin white roofs hover over the walls on slim columns or none at all, with solar cells set flush into them. Keep every roof edge at least 0.1 units thick and every column at least 0.12 units across, so they still read at phone size.
- **Clean roofs.** No rooftop clutter. Roofs are white or solar and carry the emblem. Only a building's instrument, such as a lab's dome, rises above them.
- **Sail towers.** A building may rise into one tapering sail-shaped tower, at least half as broad at its base as it is tall, never a thin needle.
- **Raised plazas.** Each building stands on a pale stone podium with lawns, a pool, or a planted court.

## Materials

Glossy white composite shells, dark reflective glass with lit floors behind fine white fins, pale stone podiums, muted lawns, and still pools. Nothing is dirty, rusted, stained, patched, or worn. Painted detail still carries every surface, so it doesn't read as a toy: fins and mullions, floors, furniture, and planting behind the glass, faint panel lines in the white, and flush solar cells in the roofs.

## Surfaces

The finish is half the look, so the atlas carries a roughness and metallic map.

| Material     | Finish                               |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| White shells | Glossy, roughness 0.25, not metallic |
| Glass        | Mirror-like, roughness 0.08          |
| Solar cells  | Glossy, roughness 0.15               |
| Fins, metal  | Satin metal, roughness 0.3, metallic |
| Stone, lawns | Matte, roughness 0.7 to 0.9          |
| Pools        | Mirror-like, roughness 0.05          |

## Glow

- **Soft glow only:** pale ice `#d6f2f0`, painted on the glow map as a soft wash that fades out, never a hard edge or a line. It lights the glass floors from inside and the pools from below, so each building looks lit through its skin.
- **Emblem and instruments:** the emblem's leaf and a lab's instrument may glow a little brighter in the same ice, still soft.
- **How much:** at most a fifth of the visible surface. Never an outline, a trim line, or a lit edge. Leave the white shells and roofs unlit so the shape reads.

## Signage

- **Emblem:** a ring around a rising leaf, invented, with the ring painted white and the leaf glowing softly. It sits at the center of each roof or courtyard and above the main entrance, and reads as a simple shape from the game camera.
- **Markings:** pale grey and white paint on paving, such as bay outlines and chevrons. No text larger than the emblem.

## References

`docs/screen_shots/inspiration/factions/advanced-utopian/structures/` holds four images of clean-energy future cities in daylight.

- **Banded glass campus.** A glass drum wrapped in white floor bands, joined to a curving slab, on lawns with curving paths. Take the curving glass, the white edges, and the lawns. Leave the stacked bands, which read as busy rather than sleek.
- **Domes and towers.** Glass domes, slender towers, and sweeping glass tubes over a lawn. Take the glass dome and the sweeping curves.
- **City of sculpted towers.** Glossy white towers with flowing vertical ribs, glass tube bridges, and turquoise canals through lawns. Take the glossy white, the flowing ribs, and the water. Leave the planted balconies stacked up the towers.
- **Swept shell station.** A single sculpted white shell with a large curved opening, fine panel lines, and a glossy reflective plaza. Take the one-gesture shell, the curved opening, and the gloss.

## Avoid

- Any dirt, grime, rust, stains, streaks, or wear, and matte surfaces that read as concrete.
- Pure white or blank surfaces with no painted detail, which read as a toy. Spotless still needs fins, mullions, and life behind the glass.
- Neon lines, lit trims, and saturated glow of any color.
- Stacked bands, stepped setbacks, and wedding-cake towers.
- Rooftop clutter: equipment, turbines, antennas, and piled parts.
- Lawns in bright green over large areas.
- Far-future pieces: floating buildings, saucer discs, needle spires, and hover vehicles.
- Thin fins, edges, and columns that vanish at phone size.
- Copies of real buildings.

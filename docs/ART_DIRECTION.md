## Reference screenshots

The screenshots come from other games, so git ignores that folder and they stay on the machine that has them. The notes below say what to take from them, so the guidance holds where the images are missing. Take the look, not the setting, factions, buildings, or UI.

### Structures

Two near-future city battles seen from a high three-quarter camera.

- **Believable proportions.** Buildings keep real-world proportions and feel heavy. Units are small next to them, and the buildings carry most of the visual weight on screen.
- **Detail comes from textures.** Forms are simple boxes, cylinders, and curved roofs with soft bevels. Window grids, panel lines, vents, concrete, and grime are painted, not modeled. Curved roofs are smooth enough that their faces don't show at game zoom.
- **Roofs identify buildings.** From this camera the roof is most of what a player sees. Barrel-vault hangars, flat roofs with rooftop equipment, and stepped towers each read differently.
- **Grounded in a lived-in setting.** Roads, lot markings, fences, pylons, trees, and parked vehicles surround the buildings, so structures sit in a place instead of on a plain.
- **A neutral environment so units stand out.** Buildings and ground should not be as bright as team colors, selection rings, and effects are the most saturated things on screen.
